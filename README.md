# 🇮🇳 India CPI Inflation Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation trends using Excel and statistical analysis.

The analysis focuses on major inflation patterns, food inflation, the impact of COVID-19, and the relationship between crude oil price fluctuations and transport-related inflation.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **Excel, data cleaning, data analysis, statistical analysis, and data visualization**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze India's CPI inflation trends over time
* Understand the contribution of major CPI categories
* Study food inflation trends
* Investigate the impact of COVID-19 on inflation
* Analyze the relationship between crude oil prices and transport inflation
* Identify important patterns and insights from CPI data

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 1. CPI Basket Contribution

The analysis examines the contribution of major CPI categories, including:

* Food & Beverages
* Clothing & Footwear
* Housing
* Fuel & Light
* Transport & Communication
* Health
* Education & Recreation
* Personal Care & Miscellaneous

**Key observation:** Food & Beverages represents the largest share of the CPI basket in the analysis.

---

### 2. Annual Inflation Trend

The project analyzes annual CPI inflation from **2017 to 2023**.

| Year | Inflation |
| ---- | --------: |
| 2017 |      3.6% |
| 2018 |      4.1% |
| 2019 |      3.7% |
| 2020 |      6.2% |
| 2021 |      5.5% |
| 2022 |      6.8% |
| 2023 |      5.2% |

**Key observation:** Inflation reached its highest level in **2022 at 6.8%**.

---

### 3. Food Inflation Analysis

Food inflation was analyzed for the 12-month period ending in May 2023.

The analysis identified:

* Highest food inflation: **7.2% in November 2022**
* Lowest food inflation: **4.8% in May 2023**

The trend shows that food inflation increased during late 2022 and gradually declined during early 2023.

---

### 4. COVID-19 Impact on Inflation

The project compares inflation before and after the onset of COVID-19 in India, using **March 2020** as the reference point.

* Average inflation before March 2020: **4.0%**
* Average inflation after March 2020: **6.1%**
* Change: **+2.1 percentage points**

The analysis indicates a significant increase in inflation after the onset of the pandemic, associated with disruptions in supply chains, essential goods, healthcare demand, and other economic factors.

---

### 5. Crude Oil Price & Transport Inflation

The project investigates how fluctuations in crude oil prices relate to transport-related inflation.

Excel's `CORREL()` function was used to measure the relationship.

**Correlation coefficient: approximately 0.989**

This indicates a **very strong positive association** between crude oil price changes and transport inflation in the analyzed data.

> Note: Correlation indicates statistical association and does not by itself prove causation.
